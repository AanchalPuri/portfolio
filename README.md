## 🪴 Welcome In

Hi, I'm Aanchal! This is my personal portfolio, a cozy little space where my two favorite hobbies live side by side: **building things with code** and **building things with clay**. 🫖✨ Welcome in!

Some days I'm debugging a Django component, other days I'm trimming a wonky bowl off the wheel. Both are about the same thing to me: taking something messy and turning it into something I'm proud of.

> *"made with patience, snacks, and probably too much tea"* 🍵

---

## 🌷 Why I Built This

I wanted one place that actually feels like *me*, not just a list of projects, but a little home for the things I make, whether it's a web app or a wobbly little mug (and I get a  corner of the internet to call my own). 🏡💌
